# Why This Repo

The goal is not to collect links. The goal is to build visible evidence of research readiness: code, experiments, notes, and communication.

For an AI research internship, a strong GitHub profile should show more than enthusiasm. It should show that I can:

- Learn hard technical material consistently.
- Translate mathematical ideas into working code.
- Compare baselines and improvements with honest metrics.
- Read research papers and identify the actual contribution.
- Communicate assumptions, limitations, and next steps.
- Work with reproducible habits: seeds, configs, environment notes, and clear experiment logs.

This repository is the central dashboard. Specialized repositories can hold deeper documentation, paper-to-code reproductions, or larger portfolio projects.
