# Experiment Design

Define hypotheses, baselines, controls, metrics, ablations, and failure analysis before coding.

## Experiment Template

### Question

What specific question does this experiment answer?

### Hypothesis

What do I expect to happen, and why?

### Baseline

What is the simplest comparison point?

### Method

What changes from the baseline?

### Data

What dataset, split, preprocessing, and known limitations are used?

### Metrics

Which metrics matter, and what failure modes do they miss?

### Controls

What stays fixed across runs?

### Ablations

Which parts of the method should be removed or varied?

### Results

Report numbers, plots, and qualitative examples.

### Interpretation

What changed, what did not change, and what is still unclear?

### Next Step

What should be tried next?
